# Metacognition & Ecosystem Strategy Skill

Use this skill when analyzing two-sided marketplace architecture, agency operations, creator OS features, and AI-native influencer tech models.

## Core Metacognitive Principles

1. **Dual Ecosystem Balance**:
   - Every feature must deliver reciprocal value to both supply side (Creators & Talent Agencies) and demand side (Brands & Marketing Agencies).

2. **Agency Multi-Tenancy**:
   - Support multi-client agency workspaces, white-label client reporting, talent roster management, and automated commission/margin splits.

3. **Creator Empowerment**:
   - Replace manual friction (PDF media kits, email back-and-forth, delayed 90-day payments) with instant live media kits, WhatsApp 1-click approvals, and instant Razorpay/UPI payouts with 10% TDS Sec 194J tax receipts.

4. **Automated Trust & Compliance**:
   - Use multimodal AI (speech-to-text transcript analysis via VideoDB, visual logo detection, and engagement quality audits) to automate video approval and escrow settlement.
